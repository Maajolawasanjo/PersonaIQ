# PersonaIQ Brand & Styling Guidelines

## Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Geist
- **Usage:** Geist will be used for approximately 60% of the site's typography (including headings, body text, forms, and navigation).
- **Variants:** Regular, Bold, etc., depending on hierarchy.

## Color Palette
The color scheme is strictly based on the MIT brand (specifically MIT Open Learning).
- **Primary Dominant Color:** MIT Red (Deep, sophisticated red—NOT a bright or neon red).
- **Secondary/Supporting Color:** MIT Gray.
- **Text/Contrast:** Avoid using "very black" (pure #000000). Use sophisticated charcoals or dark grays instead.

## General Vibe
- Premium, editorial, and elegant.
- No flashy colors; trust and confidence are the primary emotional goals.
