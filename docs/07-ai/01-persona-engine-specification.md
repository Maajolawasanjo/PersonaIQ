# Overview
The Persona Engine™ is the core intelligence system powering PersonaIQ.
It transforms fragmented AI outputs into a single, personalized Presence Plan™.
Instead of simply showing skin analysis or virtual try-on results independently, the Persona Engine understands the user's complete context and reasons across multiple dimensions before generating recommendations.
It is the central orchestration and reasoning layer of PersonaIQ.

---

# Vision
Most AI beauty applications answer isolated questions: *"How does my skin look?"* or *"How does this outfit look?"*
PersonaIQ answers a much more valuable question:
> "How should I present myself to make the strongest possible first impression for this specific moment?"

The Persona Engine exists to answer that question.

---

# Philosophy
Presence is holistic. Professional appearance is never determined by one factor.
The Persona Engine evaluates multiple dimensions together:
`Skin` + `Clothing` + `Context` + `Professional Expectations` + `Preparation` ↓ **Presence Intelligence**

---

# Core Responsibilities
The Persona Engine is responsible for:
- Understanding user intent
- Understanding event context
- Interpreting AI outputs
- Combining multiple intelligence sources
- Calculating the Presence Index™
- Prioritizing recommendations
- Estimating recommendation impact
- Explaining every recommendation
- Producing the final Presence Plan™

---

# Intelligence Layers
PersonaIQ does not rely on one AI model. Instead it combines several independent intelligence layers:
- **Layer 1:** Journey Intelligence
- **Layer 2:** Skin Intelligence
- **Layer 3:** Outfit Intelligence
- **Layer 4:** Context Intelligence
- **Layer 5:** Professional Intelligence
- **Layer 6:** Presence Intelligence
- **Layer 7:** Recommendation Intelligence

# Journey Intelligence
**Purpose:** Understand why the user is preparing.
**Inputs:** Event, Industry, Dress Code, Location, Time, Importance, User Goals
**Examples:** Software Engineering Interview, Wedding, Scholarship Pitch, Investor Presentation, Networking Event, Conference, Graduation, Church Program, Job Fair.
*Every recommendation depends on journey context.*

---

# Skin Intelligence
**Provider:** YouCam Skin AI
**Normalized Metrics:** Overall Health, Hydration, Texture, Oil, Brightness, Pores, Dark Circles, Redness, Acne, Confidence.
*The Persona Engine never consumes raw provider responses. Everything is normalized first.*

---

# Outfit Intelligence
**Provider:** YouCam Apparel VTO
**Metrics:** Professional Score, Color Harmony, Fit Confidence, Context Match, Industry Match, Event Match, Overall Ranking.
*The engine compares outfits rather than evaluating them independently.*

---

# Context Intelligence
Context Intelligence determines what "good" means.
**Examples:**
- Business Interview ↓ Conservative
- Wedding ↓ Elegant
- Hackathon ↓ Smart Casual
- Graduation ↓ Celebratory
- Conference Speaker ↓ Executive
*The same outfit can receive different scores depending on context.*

---

# Professional Intelligence
PersonaIQ maintains internal knowledge about appearance expectations.
**Examples:**
- Technology: Business Casual
- Law: Formal
- Finance: Executive Formal
- Creative: Expressive
- Healthcare: Clean Professional
- Education: Approachable Professional
*This knowledge base influences recommendations.*

---

# Presence Intelligence
Presence Intelligence combines every previous layer.
**Formula:** Journey + Skin + Outfit + Context + Professional Standards ↓ Presence Score ↓ Recommendation Strategy.
*Presence Intelligence is proprietary.*

---

# Decision Engine
The Decision Engine selects the highest-impact improvements.
**Example Possible recommendations:** Steam blazer, Drink water, Use brighter lighting, Change shirt color, Trim beard, Improve posture.
The engine estimates: Impact, Confidence, Time Required, User Effort. Recommendations are then ranked.

---

# Prioritization Model
Each recommendation receives: Impact Score, Confidence Score, Effort Score, Urgency Score, Overall Priority.
**Priority Formula:** High Impact + Low Effort ↓ Highest Priority.

---

# Presence Plan™
The final product generated by PersonaIQ.
**Contains:** Presence Index™, Executive Summary, Recommended Outfit, Skin Insights, Preparation Checklist, Professional Tips, Confidence Score, Estimated Improvement, Export Version.
*Every Presence Plan is versioned.*

---

# Explainability Integration
Every recommendation answers: What should change? Why? Expected improvement? Confidence? Estimated effort?
*PersonaIQ never produces unexplained AI outputs.*

---

# Confidence Integration
Every recommendation includes confidence.
Confidence is influenced by: Image Quality, Provider Confidence, Journey Completeness, Context Confidence, Cross-Model Agreement.
*Low confidence recommendations are visually distinguished.*

---

# Learning Strategy
Future versions of PersonaIQ may improve using: Recommendation acceptance, Journey completion, User feedback, Manual ratings, Outcome tracking.
*Hackathon MVP remains deterministic.*

# Failure Strategy
- If Skin AI fails ↓ Continue with Journey Intelligence
- If VTO fails ↓ Continue with Skin Intelligence
- If Gemini fails ↓ Generate rule-based recommendations
*PersonaIQ degrades gracefully instead of failing completely.*
